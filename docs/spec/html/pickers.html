Emoji and GIF pickers

The emoji picker and the GIF picker open from the input box and put an emoji into the draft or send a GIF. The emoji picker also opens from a message's action bar to add a reaction. On desktop each picker floats above the input box. On a phone both share one bottom sheet with two tabs. Everything inside the pickers answers at once; only GIF search and trending come from the network.

Anatomy

  1. Category bar. A 40 px rail on the left of the emoji picker, one icon per section: Recent, each custom pack, then the ten categories. The icon of the section in view is highlighted.
  2. Emoji search. A field at the top of the grid with a clear button. Results update on every keystroke.
  3. Emoji grid. Sections with a small capitals header: Recent, the custom packs, then the categories. Cells are 48 px with a 40 px image, 36 px with a 28 px image on a phone.
  4. GIF rail. A 40 px rail on the left of the GIF picker: Recent, Favorites, Server library, Trending, Browse.
  5. GIF search row. The field, its clear button, a spinner while the provider searches, and the upload button.
  6. GIF results. A two-column masonry of cards. A card keeps its GIF's aspect ratio and plays its preview. Library cards carry a favourite star.
  7. Attribution. A small "Powered by Klipy" badge in a footer strip while provider results are shown.
  8. Phone sheet. One sheet from the bottom with the tabs "GIFs" and "Emoji", half the screen high, at most 400 px.

Rules

Emoji picker

PK-01The emoji picker is 460 px wide and at most 450 px high on desktop. It has no title, no close button and no footer. It closes as the input box describes, and on Escape.

PK-02The sections are, in order: Recent, the custom packs ("Custom" from the server first, then the built-in packs A to Z), then the categories Smileys, Gestures, Animals, Nature, Food, Activities, Travel, Objects, Symbols, Flags. Each section has a header in small capitals.

PK-03The category bar has one button per section with the same order: a clock for Recent, the first image of each pack, and one emoji for each category. The button of the section at the top of the view is highlighted and follows the scroll.

PK-04A tap on a category button clears the search and scrolls the grid so that the section's header sits directly under the search field. Nothing waits for the server.

PK-05Emoji show as images from the same set as in messages, 40 px in a 48 px cell. Each emoji is in exactly one category. There is no skin tone chooser; each cell is one fixed emoji.

PK-06The search matches the typed text against each emoji's keywords and each pack emoji's shortcode. Results update on every keystroke, stay grouped under their section headers, Recent first, and sections without a hit disappear. No hit shows "No emojis found". The clear button, a category tap and a reopen all clear the search.

PK-07Recent holds the reader's last 20 emoji, newest first, without duplicates. A use is a pick in either picker, a quick reaction, or a tap on a reaction pill. Emoji typed with a keyboard do not count. The list belongs to the account and follows the reader to other devices. Empty, it reads "No recent emojis yet".

PK-08The Recent row does not move while the picker is open. It is refreshed on the next open.

PK-09Custom packs come from two places: a folder on the server, shown as one section named "Custom", and packs shipped with Yap. A pack emoji is written as ":shortcode:", the file name in lower case. Shortcodes share one namespace; a server emoji with the same name hides the shipped one. An empty pack is not shown.

PK-10A pack emoji cell has the tooltip ":shortcode:". Animated pack images animate in the cell.

PK-11Cell images load as they come into view, so a closed picker loads nothing. The picker opens at once on the first tap of the session.

PK-12On a phone, cells are 36 px with a 28 px image, and the category bar is 36 px wide.

GIF picker

PK-13The GIF picker is 500 px wide and at most 460 px high on desktop. The rail on the left has Recent, Favorites, Server library, Trending and Browse. Server library is hidden while the server has no GIFs. Trending is disabled while no provider is configured.

PK-14The first open shows Browse when a provider is configured or the reader has favourites or the server has GIFs; otherwise Recent. The picker keeps its tab and its open folder across close and open.

PK-15A tap on a tab clears the search and shows that tab with fresh data. A tap on the active tab does nothing.

PK-16Recent shows the reader's last 30 GIFs sent from the picker, newest first, without duplicates. It follows the account. Empty, it reads "No recent GIFs yet".

PK-17Trending shows the provider's trending GIFs as a grid, without a header. While they load it reads "Loading…". When they cannot be loaded it reads "Could not load GIFs" and the next open tries again.

PK-18Browse shows shortcut tiles first: a "Favorites" tile when the reader has favourites and a tile with the project name when the server has GIFs. Under them, a "Trending" header and the trending grid. With no provider, no favourites and no server GIFs it reads "Nothing here yet". A tile opens its tab.

PK-19Cards form two columns in masonry with 6 px gaps. A card keeps its GIF's aspect ratio and shows a small, muted, looping preview. A preview that cannot start plays on the next tap or key press anywhere.

PK-20A tap on a card sends the GIF at once and closes the picker, as the input box describes. The search text of that moment is remembered with the GIF as a tag, so the next search for it finds it. On a keyboard, a focused card sends on Enter or Space.

PK-21Library cards (Recent, Favorites, Server library, and library hits in a search) have a favourite star at the top right. On desktop it appears on hover; a favourited star is gold and always shows. A tap on the star changes the favourite only. Provider cards have no star; to keep a provider GIF, the reader sends it, then stars it in Recent or in the message.

PK-22Cards show no title, uploader or folder.

GIF search and the provider

PK-23The search field reads "Search Klipy..." with a provider, else "Search your library...". The clear button shows while there is text. A spinner shows while the provider is searched.

PK-24The search runs a quarter second after the last keystroke. Library hits show first under "From your library"; provider hits follow under the provider's name. An empty field shows the active tab again. No hits at all reads "No matches".

PK-25Library search matches every word of two letters or more against a GIF's tags. A GIF that misses one word may still show, at most two of them with a provider configured. Hits are ordered by matched words, then the reader's favourites, then server GIFs, then use.

PK-26A reader finds server GIFs, provider GIFs that anyone has sent, their own uploads, and GIFs they starred. Other people's uploads stay private until starred.

PK-27A GIF's tags come from the search text of each send, the provider's title and tags, the words of an uploaded file's name, and admin edits in the library.

PK-28Provider results come in pages of 30. "Load more" at the end of the list adds the next page, in Trending, Browse and search.

PK-29The provider is Klipy. It is configured with a key on the server. Without a key: Trending is disabled, the field searches the library only, there is no provider section and no attribution. The admin's content rating setting applies to search and trending.

PK-30While provider results are shown, a footer strip shows the "Powered by Klipy" badge.

PK-31A provider that does not answer gives no provider section and no error; library hits or "No matches" show instead.

Favourites and the server library

PK-32Favorites shows folder tiles first, then an "Unsorted" header when folders exist, then the unfiled favourites. Under the content, a link "Manage your GIFs in Settings". Empty, it reads "⭐ Tap the star on any GIF to save it here".

PK-33A folder tile is a 2:1 tile with a blurred, darkened GIF from the folder and the folder name in white. Which GIF is shown may change between visits. A tap opens the folder with a back row "← {folder}"; a tap on the back row returns.

PK-34Folders are one level deep, ordered A to Z. They are created and filled in Settings, not in the picker. A star from the picker files the GIF as unsorted; a star on a GIF already in a folder keeps its folder.

PK-35Favourites are ordered by the reader's most recent use, newest first. There is no limit on favourites.

PK-36A change made elsewhere (a star in a message, an edit in Settings) shows in an open picker at once. When the open folder is removed, the view returns to the root.

PK-37Server library has the same layout as Favorites, without the Settings link. Admins fill it from the Admin page; everyone sees and searches it. Empty, it reads "No server GIFs yet".

Upload from the picker

PK-38The upload button in the search row opens the file chooser for one file: .gif, .webp, .mp4, .mov or .webm. A file over 50 MB shows "Couldn't send GIF" with "File is too large (max 50MB)" and the picker stays open.

PK-39While the file uploads and is processed, the button becomes a spinner and an overlay with a spinner and "Adding GIF…" covers the results. The search row and the rail stay usable. There is no percentage.

PK-40Videos become animated images without sound. A file with the same content as an existing GIF reuses that GIF. The file name's words become the first tags.

PK-41On success the GIF joins the reader's favourites as unsorted, is sent as a message (a reply when the reply bar is open), and the picker closes. The GIF stays private to the reader until someone stars it.

PK-42On failure the picker stays open behind "Couldn't send GIF" with "Could not process this file as a GIF" when the file was refused, or "Upload failed" otherwise. The reader's storage quota applies to picker uploads as to any upload.

The reaction picker

PK-43Add Reaction in a message's action bar opens the emoji picker for that message, with the same sections, search and size. It has no quick reactions; those are in the action bar.

PK-44A pick closes the picker and adds or removes that reaction on the message, and moves the emoji to the front of Recent. Position, closing and the row's state while it is open are on the message row page.

The phone sheet

PK-45On a phone (600 px or less) both pickers live in one bottom sheet with the tabs "GIFs" and "Emoji" in that order. Emoji is the active tab on the first open. The sheet is half the screen high, at most 400 px. Each tab fills the sheet and scrolls inside it. The rails are 36 px wide.

PK-46A tab switch is instant and keeps each tab's search text, results and scroll position. The active tab is remembered until the page is reloaded.

PK-47Neither search field takes focus when the sheet opens. A tap on an emoji cell closes the phone keyboard before the emoji is inserted. A GIF card tap closes the sheet, which closes the keyboard.

PK-48The reaction picker on a phone is a sheet of the same size over a dimmed page.

Without a connection

PK-49Without a connection the emoji picker works in full. In the GIF picker, search, Trending and Browse read "Needs a connection"; Recent, Favorites and Server library show what was last loaded, and a tap on a card queues the GIF as a message like any other.
