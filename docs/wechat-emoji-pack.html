WeChat sticker pack

The 75 built-in WeChat stickers shipped in wwwroot/emoji-packs/wechat/, with the name each one answers to in Yap and what it actually means in Chinese chat. Several of them do not mean what the picture shows.

  1. Some stickers mean the opposite of what they show. Only 15.67% of people use 再见 (the wave) to actually say goodbye, and 66.41% use 微笑 (the smile) to signal “I am done with this conversation”.
  2. The split runs by age. Users born in the 2000s favour 捂脸, the 90s cohort 笑哭, the 80s cohort 呲牙. A boss's 微笑 reads as a threat to a younger employee.
  3. 旺柴 (the Shiba) is a sarcasm marker. Chinese users call it 保命 (“life insurance”): you add it so nobody mistakes your joke for a real opinion.

Smiles and laughs 13

:smile:微笑wēixiàoOlder users mean a warm hello, younger users read an icy brush-off, so it is the riskiest sticker here.
:happy:笑脸xiàoliǎnA plain open smile with no baggage, for the friendliness that 微笑 can no longer carry.
:grin:呲牙cīyáThe workhorse laugh and China's most-sent sticker for years, also used to close a chat warmly.
:goofy:憨笑hānxiàoA dopey, sincere grin, though about a fifth of users flip it into sarcasm when annoyed.
:cheerful:愉快yúkuàiSimple good mood, lighter than a laugh.
:snicker:偷笑tōuxiàoQuiet gloating, often at someone else's trouble.
:smirk:坏笑huàixiàoA naughty grin that says the sender is up to something.
:cheeky:调皮tiáopíTongue out and winking: “I'm just messing with you.”
:scheming:奸笑jiānxiàoA sly “heh heh, you know what I mean”, usually attached to a bad idea.
:sinister:阴险yīnxiǎnThe villain smile meaning “you're finished”, genuinely hostile.
:heyha:嘿哈hēihāA cocky kung-fu shout, the sound of dodging your punch and enjoying it.
:laughcry:破涕为笑pòtìwéixiàoCrying and laughing at once, the all-purpose reaction before 捂脸 replaced it.
:facepalm:捂脸wǔliǎnThe most-sent sticker in the set and the signature of Gen Z, covering “I can't look” and “that hurts from laughing”.

Search keywords live in wwwroot/emoji-packs/wechat/keywords.json, which carries the Chinese name and pinyin too, so 捂脸, wulian, cringe and facepalm all find the same sticker. Deleting a shortcode is a soft break: old messages store the literal :name: text.
